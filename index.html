Our Story

Dedicated to Your Academic Success

White Line Educational Consulting Services Company is a professional educational consulting and training organization established on September 25, 2023. We are dedicated to supporting students in achieving their academic and international education goals through high-quality guidance, training, and counseling services.

Our company specializes in a wide range of educational services, including academic consulting, scholarship application assistance, university admissions guidance, visa process support, and exam preparation programs. We work closely with students to help them successfully navigate every step of their academic journey, from application to admission and beyond.

We provide specialized English language training courses, including TOEFL preparation classes, designed to equip students with the skills and confidence needed to succeed in international English proficiency exams. We also offer structured online classes for female students in Afghanistan who are unable to attend physical classes, ensuring equal access to quality education.

Since our establishment, White Line has successfully supported numerous students in securing scholarships at reputable international universities and obtaining admissions from globally recognized institutions. Our training programs have prepared hundreds of students for TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo English Test, GRE, and other internationally recognized exams.

Our Services

What We Offer

At White Line, we provide a wide range of educational consulting and training services designed to support students in achieving their academic and international study goals.

Educational Consulting

We guide students in selecting suitable academic paths, universities, and programs based on their goals, qualifications, and future aspirations.

Scholarship Assistance

We help students identify and apply for international scholarship opportunities and support them throughout the application process to increase their chances of success.

University Admission Guidance

Our team provides step-by-step assistance for obtaining admission to reputable international universities, including document preparation and application support.

Visa Application Support

We assist students in preparing and organizing their visa applications, ensuring all required documents and procedures are properly completed.

English Language Training

We offer structured English language courses to improve students' reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills for academic and professional use.

TOEFL Preparation Courses

We provide specialized TOEFL preparation classes designed to help students achieve their target scores through focused training, practice tests, and exam strategies.

Online Learning Programs

We offer online classes for students, especially for female students in Afghanistan who cannot attend physical classes, ensuring accessible and flexible education for everyone.

International Exam Preparation

We prepare students for globally recognized exams such as TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo English Test, GRE, and others, helping them achieve competitive scores.

Our Objectives

What We Stand For

Every objective we pursue is rooted in one core belief — every student deserves access to world-class education and the opportunity to succeed globally.

01

Support Students Pursuing Higher Education Abroad

To support and guide students who aspire to continue their higher education in foreign countries by providing professional educational consulting and academic planning services.

02

Help Students Secure International Scholarships

To assist students in identifying and applying for international scholarship opportunities and to increase their chances of obtaining fully funded and partially funded scholarships.

03

Provide High-Quality English Language Training

To provide high-quality English language training programs that improve students' academic and communication skills for international education and professional success.

04

Prepare Students for TOEFL, IELTS, GRE & Duolingo

To prepare students for internationally recognized examinations such as TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo English Test, GRE, and other standardized tests through effective training and exam preparation programs.

05

Support Exam Bookings and Academic Procedures

To support students financially and administratively in booking TOEFL and other international examinations whenever possible.

06

Provide Affordable Learning Opportunities

To facilitate access to educational opportunities by providing exam fee discounts, voucher support, and affordable learning services for deserving students.

07

Offer Accessible Online Learning for Afghan Students

To create accessible online learning opportunities, especially for students in Afghanistan, including female students who may face limitations in attending physical classes.

08

Empower Afghan Students Through Global Opportunities

To empower Afghan students to achieve their academic and career goals by connecting them with global educational opportunities and international institutions.

Our Core Commitment

Every student who walks through our doors — physically or virtually — deserves the same level of dedication, expertise, and care in their educational journey.
